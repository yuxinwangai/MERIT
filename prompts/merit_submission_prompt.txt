Task
Based on the provided 3 × 3 axial brain CTA panel and the corresponding clinical information, generate a co-ordered 3 × 3 array of synthetic CT perfusion Tmax-like parameter maps.
Each output tile must correspond tile-by-tile to the CTA slice at the same position in the input panel. Preserve the original panel order and cranio-caudal sequence. Do not reorder, duplicate, merge, omit, or substitute output tiles.
The generated maps must emulate clinical CT perfusion Tmax post-processing maps. They should visualize broad parenchymal perfusion-delay patterns rather than vessels, angiographic enhancement, or other perfusion parameters.
Tmax refers to the time to maximum of the residue function. The generated output is a synthetic Tmax-like visual representation and must not be interpreted as a quantitative Tmax measurement in seconds.

Core principle
Synthetic Tmax-like signal must remain strictly confined to the valid brain-parenchymal mask of each corresponding CTA tile. It must never exceed the effective tissue extent visible on CTA.
